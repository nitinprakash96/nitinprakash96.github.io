I am planning an **11-day motorcycle ride through Central Mongolia** and looking for a guide who knows the routes well.

### Basic Plan

- **Riding duration:** 11 days
- **Start:** Ulaanbaatar
- **Finish:** Ulaanbaatar
- **Expected terrain:** Mixed highway and off-road
- **Accommodation preference:** Yurt stays/camping where possible (willing to rent camping gear if camping is an option)
- **Navigation:** I would like to confirm the actual route, distances, fuel stops, and overnight stops with you before starting

### Proposed 11-Day Riding Itinerary

| Ride Day | Route / Stay | Notes | Approx. Distance |
|---|---|---|---:|
| Day 1 | Ulaanbaatar → Genghis Khan Statue → Khustai National Park | Leave Ulaanbaatar and begin the ride. Visit Genghis Khan Statue if practical, then continue toward Khustai. | ~180–220 km |
| Day 2 | Khustai → Ugii Lake | Mixed off-road and road riding. Stay near Ugii Lake. | ~150–170 km |
| Day 3 | Ugii Lake → Taikhar Stone | Ride toward Taikhar Stone and stay nearby. | ~140–220 km |
| Day 4 | Taikhar Rock → Terkh White Lake | Long scenic riding day toward Terkhiin Tsagaan Lake. | ~170–200 km |
| Day 5 | Rest / local exploration at Terkh White Lake | Rest day. Possible volcano hike/local exploration. No major distance planned. | Local only |
| Day 6 | Terkhiin White Lake → Chuluut River / Arkhangai backcountry | Adventure-focused riding day through quieter terrain instead of the more touristy hot springs stop. Exact overnight location to be confirmed with the guide. | ~120–180 km |
| Day 7 | Chuluut River / Arkhangai backcountry → Orkhon Waterfall | Likely one of the more difficult off-road days, with volcanic rock, rough tracks, and possible river crossings depending on the route. | ~160–220 km |
| Day 8 | Orkhon Waterfall / Orkhon Valley exploration | Explore the waterfall and valley. Optional short ride or rest depending on conditions. | Local only |
| Day 9 | Orkhon Waterfall → Kharkhorin | Ride to Kharkhorin, Mongolia’s ancient capital. | ~100–130 km |
| Day 10 | Kharkhorin → Elsen Tasarkhai / Mini Gobi | Visit monastery/museum if practical, then ride toward the sand dunes. | ~80–100 km |
| Day 11 | Elsen Tasarkhai → Ulaanbaatar | Return to Ulaanbaatar and finish the ride. | ~270–300 km |

### Important Notes

- I am very comfortable riding motorcycles.
- But at the same time I want to ride safely and not rush the route.
- I understand Mongolia can have difficult weather and rough off-road tracks, so I want to plan properly. If something feels off in the route, please let me know.
- The itinerary can be adjusted based on your local knowledge and the real condition of the roads/tracks.
